### Research

I build transformer foundation models for **long, irregularly sampled clinical event sequences**,
and study how their learned representations can predict outcomes, reveal patient subgroups and explain risk.

- **EHR foundation models.** Pretrained CoreBEHRT-based transformers on the health trajectories of ~2.2M patients
  from Danish nationwide registries; multi-task prediction of 21 postoperative complications.
- **Medical imaging.** Seven years of deep learning for MRI, ultrasound, retinal imaging and radiographs,
  including a foundation model for bone radiographs.
- **Multimodal clinical AI.** Combining imaging with longitudinal health records (DTU Compute).
